Damien's Public Gournal 1

August 3, 2013:
I packed some grapes and a few cookies in my cargo pockets and took the bus to the Walmart where I intended to investigate what the radio called "the best contraceptive ever!" This "contraceptive" is a pair of stockings that make a woman's legs look like they are covered in thick,
coarse hair.
They are definitely not a contraceptive, but are intended to keep weirdos and perverts at bay.
Don't girls know that the most perverted weirdos prefer hairy legs?

As I walked through the parking lot, I noticed a woman parking in the "expectant mother" spot.
I thought, does that mean anyone who is expecting to have a child one day is allowed to park there?
No, that is not the case; the spot is only for those who are currently pregnant.

The woman parked her car and walked ahead of me into Walmart.
She was heavyset with red hair and extensive freckles, as if someone threw mud at her through a screen door.
Her pants were tight on her hips and I could make out the shape of her panties: they were "grandmother" panties.

I thought, what if she isn't pregnant?
What if she is only fat?
That's not for me to judge so I decided to let it go.
But as we entered the store, she walked in the same direction as me, towards the "female products".

I am only looking for stockings covered in thick hair to see if they are real and if they come in different colors for girls with different colored hair,
and to see what ingredients are used to make the hair; are they made with real hair?
I am not following the "pregnant woman".

She turns down an aisle and I walk past her to the "feminine garments" section of the store.
I find the isle where stockings and pantyhose are stocked.
I do not see any stockings with hair.
I can't ask for assistance because nobody at Walmart ever shows you where something is,
they just point and say it's on the isle where you were a minute ago.

I walk around to see if they are stocked on an end cap,
and I see the "pregnant woman" looking at some panties that my grandmother would never wear.
She might know about the stockings, so I walk towards her, but before I have a chance to ask my question,
I am caught by the contents of her little Walmart basket: tampons!
(Pregnant women do not use tampons because they do not have a period because a sperm fertilized their egg,
and although some women do experience vaginal bleeding during pregnancy,
they would never use tampons to stop the bleeding because of the possibility of Toxic Shock Syndrome,
which sounds terrifying)

I asked her, "Are you menstruating?" She seemed toxically shocked by the question at first,
but after a few seconds she reluctantly answered and said, "yes,
why do you ask?" "That means you aren't pregnant!" I exclaimed.
She patted me on the head and said, "that's right, but the lord knows I am trying." I hate being patted on the head.

"Well, you shouldn't've parked in the expectant mother spot if you are not pregnant."

She was silent for a few more seconds.
"Excuse me?
Expectant mother spot?" Yes, I saw you park in the expectant mother parking spot.
Pregnancy is a very serious condition.
I won't report you this time, but next time, you better believe I will."

She knelt down and she exhaled a little grunt, which means it was not easy for her to bend down that low.
She put her face very close to mine and said, "next time, I will be pregnant."

She grunted again as she stood and put the non-grandmother panties in her basket, and then she walked away.

I wish I would've asked her where the hairy stockings were before I asked her if she was menstruating.
