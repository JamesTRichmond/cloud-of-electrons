Machines are Fighting for our Freedom

Flying at super-sonic speeds, it's hard for anyone to see them. It's even harder to imagine just how anyone could beat them.

Machines, Machines, Machines are Fighting for our Freedom.

While kids are herding goats, a mother goes out to meet them. But instead of finding children, she finds a missile there to greet them.

Machines, Machines, Machines are Fighting for our Freedom.

Bible and flag in his hand, the politician swears that we need them. "They're fast, they're effective, and we don't even have to feed them!"

Machines, Machines, Machines are Fighting for our Freedom.

Soldiers' names sound so noble when they're dead and politicians read them. "Have no fear, we'll bring them home, the machine is always here to lead them!"

Machines, Machines, Machines are Fighting for our Freedom.
