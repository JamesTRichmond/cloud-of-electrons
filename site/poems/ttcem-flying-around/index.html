Flying Around

The things that try to kill me: Flashing zippy gut splatterers, sticky spiral graveyards, plain old dehydration, and a lumbering beast who guards their slop, infuriated with me and the air around me. They swing slowly and wide. While jumping, while climbing. The stench calls to me. From lamp to cupboard to ceiling, back to lamp and to ceiling again — hide in a shadow. SWAT! A lousy swing. CRACK! The room goes quiet. I land on the nicest pile of shit. A pixelated buffet begging for my barf.¹ Oh fuck! My labella ignites with pleasure. I ate, I drank, and in the warmth of it all, I laid there, no hurry, buzzing. The swatting ended cold, and nothing crashed against me.

A light reached out, lured me up from the shit, i threw myself against its warmth, hard, and for so long I forgot why I began to try. A pungent stench, loud as life, the rot of the world, stole my attention from the warmth.² My sweets will eat a feast. Dried up a heartbeat later, revolting but unaware, ready to fly around again.
