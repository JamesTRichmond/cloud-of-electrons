Metta in Vermont

There's a zen meditation center in my little town, and I joined a metta session this afternoon.

The monk is sweet and invites the community to join (or to just come and go or do whatever), and some people take advantage of that.

A group of people came in for the coffee and snacks and had a side conversation in the room during the meditation. Most of it was incomprehensible, but some of it was loud enough to make out. For instance, one of them was describing a person he knew who could catch arrows in mid flight.

I felt myself getting annoyed, but I also had a funny revelation.

I've meditated outside before, and the birds were chirping much more than these people were talking.

I thought, what if I knew what the birds were saying?
