Jagged Rocks

You

should know,

I dream of you naked, thrashing on a bed of roses. Are you smelling all the flowers? Are you bleeding from all the thistles? Are you planted with the bushes in the dirt?

Your smile is borne by your haunting gaze, your hair is terra cotta. Your bangs fall uneven across your face, chaotic as if chopped with a hatchet. Your eyes are baked crockeries formed from china clay, your irises crystal glazed celadon.

Art is on your hands, on your fingertips. Torn from forming, scabbed from mourning, your hands become the art your art creates: mended fabric, sewn and worn, canvas brushed and spattered, cupcakes iced and sprinkled.

Your fingers are figures painted naked and shameless, sometimes serious, sometimes playful.

Your freckles are constellations used to navigate the body; vessels are charting, disregarding the jagged rocks.

See it form in the cloud
