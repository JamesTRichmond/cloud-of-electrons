Sunshine

i love sunshine. i'm going to miss her while she's gone. i'll think of her periodically because i'll see someone standing on their hands.

i'll call her and leave a message and i hope she smiles when she hears it.

i hope she has a great trip and learns a lot and makes a lot of people fall in love with her and falls in love a couple times, too and experiences nature in a new way or at least comes to some new way of thinking about nature.

i hope she eats something exotic.

i love sunshine. i'm going to miss her while she's gone.
