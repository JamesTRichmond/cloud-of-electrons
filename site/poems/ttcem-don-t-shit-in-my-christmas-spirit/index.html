Don't Shit in My Christmas Spirit

Shrouded in darkness, the blankets crept to the top of the bed, over the head of the innocent.

Shrouded in darkness, the Anus expelled the fart, filling the blanket to its bitter brim of capacity.

Shrouded In Darkness! the innocent was smothered, snuffed out by an Anus and a fart.

See it form in the cloud
