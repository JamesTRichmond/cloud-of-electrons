# Cloud of Electrons: Style Frame v1 (Art Director)

Read from the Poetry & Prose folder: the poems are night-time, weather-struck, earthy, funny, and raw (lightning over graves, crickets in the dark, stardust, a fly's-eye view, Bukowski-style plain talk).

Mood: late night after a storm. Dark, wet, electric, a little profane, never slick.

Palette
- Ground: #05070D near-black blue. No pure black, no background gradients.
- Poem text: #EDE6D6 warm off-white, the only non-spot color. Plain serif. No glow beyond a soft 0.15 bloom.
- Lightning accent: #F4F7FF, used ONLY at peak coherence (the "FLASH!" moment), max 1 per 60 s.
- Spots (placeholders): Brain Fog 270 violet, AgentiCubed 190 cyan, Preincarnation 150 marble green-gray, Verbal Kombat 5 red, Habitat/Aural 38 amber.
- Each spot's saturation stays under 0.7, so the poem text always reads brighter than the spot around it.

Texture and motion
- Particles drift like rain-lit dust, never sparkles or confetti.
- Haze = fog after rain: low, wet, slow.
- Poems gather from the haze nearest them and take a faint tint of that spot's hue in the first 200 ms, then settle to off-white.

Type
- One serif family, two weights. Keep J R's original line breaks and lowercase/uppercase exactly.
- Line length max ~42 characters per row of particles. Long lines wrap only at J R's own breaks, or split into groups.

Don'ts
- No stock "AI brain" imagery, neurons, circuit boards, or human faces.
- No neon cyberpunk palette.
- No text over a bright spot core.
